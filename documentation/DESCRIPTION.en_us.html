Coddy Round is Audiowide with every sharp outer corner rounded, the way the Coddy wordmark was drawn: the weight, proportions and curves are Audiowide's own, and each outer corner is filleted with a radius of half the stem, so every stroke ends in a half circle while the inner corners stay crisp.

The family has five weights, from Regular to ExtraBold, and covers Latin and Cyrillic. Its Cyrillic letters are drawn from Audiowide's own parts and stroke, and its heavier weights keep Audiowide's designed slits open.

Coddy Round is a Modified Version of Audiowide by Astigmatic (Brian J. Bonislawsky), made by Coddy.
